# Full Paper Machinist (FPM)

**Researcher-directed scientific work with AI — public documentation preview**

[Português](README.pt-BR.md) · [Get started](GET_STARTED.md) · [Method](docs/METHOD.md) · [Architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Limitations](docs/LIMITATIONS.md) · [Roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md)

Full Paper Machinist (FPM) is a project for making collaboration between researchers and AI systems more **useful, transparent, and scientifically accountable**. Its focus is to turn an interesting question into testable alternatives, literature-grounded reasoning, manageable computational experiments, and claims that do not outrun the evidence.

The researcher defines the scientific direction. AI can help with exploration, modelling, code, analysis, and writing **only when the relevant tools and evidence are actually available**. Human decisions, executed experiments, and independent checks remain distinct from AI-generated suggestions.

## Start here

This public repository currently contains **documentation, not a downloadable FPM application or Autoboot release**.

**[Try a guided research conversation](GET_STARTED.md)** with an AI of your choice. This is a short example you can use right now, not the full FPM Autoboot package.

## Scientific principles

- **Question before infrastructure:** identify what the researcher wants to understand.
- **Alternatives and controls:** compare mechanisms and consider informative negative results.
- **Evidence before claims:** distinguish hypotheses, proposed tests, executed work, observations, validation and interpretation.
- **Proportionate documentation:** add records or checkpoints when continuity and reproducibility genuinely require them.
- **Human scientific authority:** researchers retain decisions about scope, interpretation and publication.

FPM is **not** a certified autonomous scientist, a cloud compute service, a guarantee of scientific validity or originality, or a substitute for peer review.
